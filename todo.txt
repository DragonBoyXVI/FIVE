
MAIN GAME LOOP
-- Pregame steps
- Game asks player for their language if its their first time playing
- Player sees title screen
- Player sees the main menu
- Player picks a character to follow
- Player may modify difficulty options if they wish, they should be editable anytime, as difficulty is not the point.
-- Main game loop
- Player should go through these stages of gameplay:
	Introductarty story
	Intro to area
	set player free to explore area and seek out the main goal/side goals
	Intermediate story as objectives are cleared
	Area climax/wrap up
	Character development as we set up the next area
- The previous stages should be modular enough to shake up for later sections
- Collectables and secrets scattered EVERYWHERE
- Repeat
- Story ends at some defining climax
-- Postgame steps
- Player sees blorbo climax (coooooommmmm)
- Player sees credits
- Player sees unlockables being unlocked
- Player has access to extras
- Repeat
